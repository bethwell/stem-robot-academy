The MiniMakers Journey

Our progressive path designed to take a child from curious builder to independent innovator.

Step 1: Explore & Discover

Learning the laws of physical motion, basic structural design, balance, and simple machine principles.

Step 2: Automate & Code

Introducing motors, electronic loops, logic sequencing, switches, and responsive behavior trees.

Step 3: Innovate & Create

Working in teams to tackle open-ended problem briefs, engineering custom functional solutions, and presenting projects to peers.

Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

Making tech education equitable and widely accessible.

We strongly believe that the future of engineering education belongs to every child. Through close partnerships with community centers, nonprofit institutions, and corporate sponsors, we sponsor free hands-on STEM introductory workshops and donate robotics hardware packages to underserved or under-resourced learning environments.
